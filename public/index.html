About the clinic

According to the project brief, Makhado Community Health Centre is a public primary healthcare facility located in Dzanani, within the Makhado Local Municipality in the Vhembe District of Limpopo Province, South Africa.

The assignment identifies primary healthcare consultations, maternal and child healthcare, chronic disease management, HIV and TB treatment, family planning, health promotion and emergency primary care among its services.

Vision and mission

Vision: To deliver quality healthcare through digital innovation.

Mission: To enhance patient care by implementing efficient and reliable healthcare information systems.

Project objectives

  • ✓Improve patient record maintenance.
  • ✓Improve appointment management.
  • ✓Reduce manual administrative work.
  • ✓Strengthen staff coordination and accountability.
